{{APP_NAME}} is a live code-share IDE for technical interviews. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Interviewers (Google sign-in): your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We request only the openid, email, and profile scopes. We never see your Google password, and we don't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or calendar.
  • Candidates: no account is needed. We store the candidate name the interviewer typed when creating the session.
  • Session content: files, code, and run output (stdout, stderr, exit codes) created during a session, plus a change history used to sync the editor between participants.
  • Technical data: request logs (method, path, status, and timing). Our hosting provider may also keep standard access logs, including IP addresses.

How we use it

  • To sign interviewers in and show their name and avatar.
  • To run interview sessions, sync edits, execute code, and let interviewers review past sessions.
  • To keep the service secure and diagnose problems.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use session content to train models.

Cookies and local storage

We use one signed session cookie to keep interviewers signed in. Your browser's local storage holds your theme preference, the Live refresh setting, and a candidate's session access token. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Where data is stored

The app runs on Vercel in the United States (us-east). Data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database. Code runs in temporary server workspaces that are discarded when the server instance is recycled.

Who can see a session

Only the interviewer who created a session, and anyone holding its PIN while the session is active, can open it. Ending a session disables its PIN. The interviewer can still review it afterward.

Retention and deletion

Sessions are kept until the interviewer asks us to delete them. To delete your account data or a specific session, or to request a copy of your data, contact us. We delete the requested data within 30 days. You can also revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

{{APP_NAME}} is meant for professional hiring and isn't directed at children under 13.
