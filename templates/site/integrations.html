Integrations

Your ATS does the scheduling. We do the rest.

Connect once in workspace settings. Interviews get a session automatically, candidates get the link in their invite, and results land back on their profile.

Ashby

Live
  • Sessions created from interview schedules through signed webhooks
  • Join link written to a candidate field for email templates
  • Results, the final code (zip), and playback posted back
  • Take-homes through Ashby's Assessments framework

ModernLoop

Live
  • Polls scheduled interviews every 10 minutes
  • Creates a session and adds the interviewers
  • Cleans up when an interview is cancelled
  • Skips candidates Ashby already handles

Public API

Live

Create a pad from any scheduler, ATS, or script and get a candidate join link back.

curl -X POST https://{{ root_domain }}/v1/pads \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer wmc_acme.…" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"candidate_name": "Ada Lovelace",
       "language": "python"}'

Returns join_url, interviewer_url, and results once the pad ends.
